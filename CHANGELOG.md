# Changelog

New, changed, fixed or removed features, one line each, brief and direct. The section for a release becomes the release notes.

## Next

- Changed: with several AD-342 'Hot Dog' drones in a squad, every drone fires flames from the first frame. Before, a second drone could fire bullets.

## 1.1.0

- New: AD-342 'Hot Dog', a Guard Dog drone that fires the flames of the FLAM-24 'Pyro' sidearm, with the Mk2 and Mk3 levels of the Guard Dog.
- Changed: the support weapon of a dead SEAF Unit soldier is deleted at once instead of staying on the ground.
- Changed: SEAF Unit needs Obsidian API 1.48.0.

## 1.0.0

- New: SEAF Unit, soldiers who follow the squad, fight, help up the fallen and collect samples. Mk2 adds a random support weapon and perk, Mk3 calls two soldiers.
- New: Homing Payload, a barrage of homing missiles from above the beacon, each aimed at an enemy near the beacon.
- New: A/MG-15 Perforator Turret, a Railcannon Turret with a mounted Justice that fires a stream of Justice rounds with no spread.
