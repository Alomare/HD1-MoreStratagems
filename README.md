# More Stratagems

Three new stratagems for HELLDIVERS (2015), for [Obsidian Mod Manager](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianModManager).

## SEAF Unit

Calls down soldiers of the Super Earth Armed Forces in slate-blue armour. They follow the squad, fight, help up the fallen and collect samples. Objectives stay with the players. A fallen soldier is not reinforced. If the player who called them leaves, the soldiers pass to another player with the mod.

- Code: ← ↓ ↓ ↑ → ←
- Unlocks at rank 5. Call-in 15 seconds, cooldown 200 seconds, 2 uses per mission.
- Mk1: one soldier with the Liberator.
- Mk2: the soldier also carries a random support weapon and a random perk: Displacement Field, Heavy Armor, Laser Aim Module, Stun Grenades or Smoke Grenades.
- Mk3: two soldiers per use, with the Mk2 kit.

On snow missions the soldiers wear All Terrain Boots when the player who called them owns that pack.

## Homing Payload

Homing missiles fall one after another from high above the beacon, and each one flies at an enemy near the beacon. The blast hurts Helldivers too.

- Code: → ↓ ↓ → ↓
- Unlocks at rank 7. Call-in 1.5 seconds, shortened by Precision Call-In.

| Level | Missiles | Interval | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mk1 | 5 | 0.5 s | 90 s |
| Mk2 | 5 | 0.5 s | 75 s |
| Mk3 | 7 | 0.4 s | 75 s |

## A/MG-15 Perforator Turret

The Railcannon Turret with an AR-20L Justice mounted on top, firing a continuous stream of Justice rounds with no spread. The rounds do not pierce heavy armor. Unlocks with the Railcannon Turret.

## Option

**SEAF custom armour** (on by default) dresses the soldiers in the slate-blue set, with no cape. Changing it needs a full deploy in Obsidian. With the option off they wear the Snow armour, and other players still see a cape.

## Playing with others

Every player in the lobby needs More Stratagems, at the same version and with the same option. In a lobby where someone lacks the mod, each new stratagem plays as the game stratagem it is built on.

Needs Obsidian API 1.48.0 or later.
