# More Stratagems

Four new stratagems for HELLDIVERS (2015), for the [Obsidian](https://github.com/Alomare/ObsidianLauncher) mod manager.

## SEAF Unit

Calls down Super Earth Armed Forces soldiers in slate-blue armour. They follow the squad, fight, help up fallen Helldivers and collect samples. Objectives stay with the players. Fallen soldiers are not reinforced. If the player who called them leaves, the soldiers pass to another player with the mod.

- Code: ← ↓ ↓ ↑ → ←
- Unlocks at rank 5. 15 second call-in, 200 second cooldown, 2 uses per mission.
- Mk1: 1 soldier with a Liberator.
- Mk2: the soldier also carries a random support weapon and a random perk: Displacement Field, Heavy Armor, Laser Aim Module, Stun Grenades or Smoke Grenades.
- Mk3: 2 soldiers per use, with the Mk2 kit.

On snow missions, soldiers wear All Terrain Boots if the player who called them owns that pack.

## Homing Payload

Homing missiles fall one by one from high above the beacon, and each flies at an enemy near the beacon. The blasts hurt Helldivers too.

## AD-342 'Hot Dog'

The AD-334 'Guard Dog' drone and backpack, armed with the flamer of the FLAM-24 'Pyro' sidearm instead of the submachine gun. Flames burn enemies, leave fire patches on the ground and hurt Helldivers too. The drone refuels in the backpack every 50 rounds, as much as the Pyro holds. Unlocks with the Guard Dog, so it needs the Defender pack.

- Code: ↓ ↑ ← ↑ → →
- 3 second call-in, 1 use, like the Guard Dog.
- Mk1: the flamer, aimed at enemies within 8 metres, the reach of the flame.
- Mk2: the drone moves while firing.
- Mk3: flames also pierce armour and briefly stun, like Guard Dog Mk3 rounds.

## Option

**SEAF custom armour** (on by default) dresses soldiers in the slate-blue set, with no cape. Changing it needs a full deploy in Obsidian. With the option off, soldiers wear the Snow armour, and other players still see a cape.

## Playing with others

Every player in the lobby needs More Stratagems, at the same version and with the same option. In lobbies where someone lacks the mod, each new stratagem plays as the base game stratagem it is built on.

Needs Obsidian API 1.48.0 or later.
